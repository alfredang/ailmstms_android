# Google Play listing

Name: TIA TMS

Short description: Classes, courseware, attendance and assessments for learners and trainers.

Full description:
TIA TMS connects Tertiary Infotech Academy learners and trainers to their classes: courseware, schedules, attendance, assessments and feedback in one place.

SIGN IN
Use your registered email address to receive a one-time sign-in code, or sign in with your password. This app is for existing learner and trainer accounts. It does not create new accounts.

YOUR CLASSES
Open a class to see its dates, venue, status and sessions, and to join an online class.

COURSEWARE IN THE APP
Slides, learner guides and activities open inside the app when they are PDFs, with zoom and page numbers. Other materials open in your browser. Some materials may require Google Drive sign-in.

ATTENDANCE
Learners scan the class QR code to check in, or open SSG e-attendance with Singpass. Trainers show the check-in QR code for a session and can mark attendance by hand.

ASSESSMENTS
Learners view the question paper and submit their files. Trainers see each learner's submissions count and record Competent or Not Yet Competent.

COURSE FEEDBACK
Send your course feedback in the app and open the TRAQOM survey.

CLASS CALENDAR AND REMINDERS
Browse your classes by month in Singapore time and add a class to your device calendar. Opt in to reminders three days and one day before each class and on the morning of class. Delivery requires notification permission.

YOUR PROFILE
Update your name and contact details, and choose a light, dark or system theme.

LEARNER AND TRAINER SPACES
Switch between your learner and trainer roles when both are assigned to your account. Administrative and finance functions are not included.

EXPLORE FIRST
Use the clearly labelled demo to explore every screen with fictional data, without signing in.

# App access instructions

No account is needed. On the sign-in screen tap Explore demo (scroll if needed). No email, password or code is required, and the DEMO banner marks the data as fictional. Nothing in the demo uses the network or changes real records.

Learner (default):
1. Home shows upcoming classes. Open Courses and tap Building AI Applications to open the class.
2. Under Courseware tap Learner slides: a sample PDF opens inside the app (pinch or use + and - to zoom).
3. Under Attendance tap Scan class QR: the demo simulates a successful check-in without opening the camera. Signed-in accounts use the Google code scanner; the app has no camera permission.
4. Tap Assessments: View question paper opens a sample PDF; Submit file opens the system file picker and simulates the upload (the chosen file is not read or sent in the demo).
5. Tap Give course feedback, rate with the stars and tap Send feedback.
6. Calendar: tap a class to open it.

Trainer: in Account tap Trainer, then open the same class. Tap QR code & attendance on a session to see the check-in QR code and mark learners Present or Absent. Tap Assessments & results to record Competent or Not Yet Competent.

Account also has Edit profile, the theme picker and Change password (nothing is changed in the demo). Turn on Class reminders and allow notifications to receive a labelled sample reminder; tap it to open Calendar. No purchases or premium content.

# Saved data safety declarations

Collected, not shared under the service-provider exemption: email address, user IDs, other information (optional training details in reminder payloads), and device or other IDs. Email/user IDs are required for app functionality, account management and security; device identifiers are required for app functionality because Firebase initializes automatically. Training details in reminder payloads are optional. All are non-ephemeral and encrypted in transit. No advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Changes to declare for 1.1.0

Review the Data safety form before releasing 1.1.0. This version adds:

- **Personal info: Name** (collected, required for app functionality and account management): shown from the account and now editable in Edit profile.
- **Personal info: Phone number** (collected, optional, app functionality): editable in Edit profile.
- **Personal info: Other info** (collected, optional): company, employment status, LinkedIn link in Edit profile.
- **Files and docs** (collected, optional, app functionality): assessment files the learner chooses to submit. They are sent to the training provider's system and stored in its Google Drive for the assessor.
- **App activity: Other user-generated content** (collected, optional, app functionality): course feedback answers, attendance check-ins and trainer attendance marks/results.
- Passwords are sent over TLS for sign-in and are not stored on the device.

Still true: encrypted in transit; no data sold; no advertising or analytics SDKs; not shared with third parties beyond service providers. The app requests no camera, storage, location or contacts permission.
